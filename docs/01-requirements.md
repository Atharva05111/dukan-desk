# Requirements — Dukan Desk (Restaurant / Cafe / Retail)

Reference: "Table" by Vyapar (billing, KOT, staff, reports, menu) — we're building a similar
but broader app that also targets **retail shops**, with **AI-assisted menu creation** and
an **AI business assistant (RAG)** for answering owner questions like "what's my profit
this month?".

## 1. Target Users

| Persona | Business type | Core need |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurant/Cafe owner | Dine-in, takeaway | Menu, KOT, table management, staff billing |
| Retail shop owner | Kirana, boutique, general store | SKU/barcode inventory, quick billing, stock valuation |
| Manager/Cashier (staff) | Both | Fast billing, limited access (role-based) |
| Accountant (optional future role) | Both | Reports, P&L, expenses |

## 2. Functional Requirements

### 2.1 Onboarding & Business Setup
- Sign up via phone/OTP or email, business name, business type (Restaurant / Cafe / Retail), GST number (optional).
- Multi-outlet support under one owner account (e.g., 2 restaurant branches).
- Choose applicable modules at setup (e.g., "Table Management" only shown for restaurant/cafe).

### 2.2 Menu / Catalog Management
- Create categories (Starter, Thali, Beverages, etc.) and items with price, image, tax rate, variants (size/spice level), add-ons.
- For retail: SKU, barcode, unit (pcs/kg/litre), HSN code, MRP vs selling price.
- **AI Menu Scan**: owner takes a photo of a physical menu card / price list → Gemini Vision extracts item names, prices, categories → shown as an editable review list → owner confirms → items are bulk-created.

### 2.3 Inventory & Stock Management
- Manual stock-in entry: item, quantity **with unit** (kg, g, litre, pcs, dozen), rate per unit, supplier (optional), date.
  - Example: "Mutton — 10 kg @ ₹650/kg" → auto-computes total cost (₹6,500) and updates stock ledger.
- Stock-out is auto-deducted when linked to a recipe/menu item (e.g., selling "Mutton Fry" deducts a configured quantity of raw mutton) or manually for wastage/spoilage.
- Low-stock threshold alerts, expiry-date tracking (for perishables), wastage logging with reason.
- Stock valuation report (current stock value at cost).
- Full stock ledger/history per item (audit trail of every stock-in / stock-out).

### 2.4 Billing / POS
- Restaurant/Cafe: table selection (or takeaway/delivery), add items, hold order, send **KOT** to kitchen printer, generate bill, split bill, apply discount, multiple payment modes (cash/UPI/card), print/share bill.
- Retail: barcode scan or search, cart, quick billing, returns/exchange.
- Order statuses: Open → On Hold → KOT Sent → Billed → Closed / Cancelled.

### 2.5 Staff & Roles
- Add staff with roles: Owner, Manager, Cashier, Waiter, Kitchen.
- Role-based permissions (e.g., waiter can only take orders, not view reports).
- Attendance / shift log (optional v2).

### 2.6 Reports & Analytics
- Today's sales, orders, top-selling items (as in reference screens).
- Daily/weekly/monthly sales, category-wise sales, payment-mode-wise breakdown.
- Inventory consumption report, wastage report.
- **Profit & Loss statement**: Revenue − COGS − Operating Expenses = Net Profit, computed for any date range.
- Expense tracking module (rent, salaries, utilities, misc.) feeding into P&L.

### 2.7 AI Business Assistant (RAG)
- Chat-style interface where the owner can ask natural-language questions:
  - "What was my profit last month?"
  - "Which item sold the most this week?"
  - "How much did I spend on mutton this month?"
  - "Explain what gross margin means for my business."
- Answers are generated by retrieving the owner's actual sales/inventory/expense data (RAG) and passing it to an LLM (Gemini) — not generic answers.
- Must clearly show numbers came from their own data, and cite the date range used.

### 2.8 Notifications
- Low stock alert, daily sales summary, staff clock-in/out (future), payment received.

## 3. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Offline-first billing**: app must allow creating orders/bills without internet, syncing when back online (critical for shops with poor connectivity).
- **Multi-tenancy**: strict data isolation per business/outlet at the database level.
- **Security**: encrypted credentials, JWT-based auth, RBAC, audit logs for stock/financial changes.
- **Performance**: billing screen must respond instantly even with 1000+ menu items.
- **Localization**: ₹ currency, GST/tax slabs (5/12/18/28%), Indian date/number formats; architecture should not hard-block future multi-currency.
- **Scalability**: architecture should support thousands of businesses (multi-tenant SaaS).
- **Platform**: Android + iOS via React Native; backend as a REST/GraphQL API.

## 4. Out of Scope (v1)
- Full accounting/ledger software replacement (Tally-style double-entry books).
- E-commerce/online ordering storefront (can be v2).
- Payroll processing.
- Hardware integrations beyond thermal printer (e.g., biometric attendance) — v2.
